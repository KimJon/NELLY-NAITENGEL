# Nelly Naitengel

**Medical Social Worker | Gospel Artist | Minister | Businesswoman | Community Advocate**

Nelly Naitengel is a multifaceted leader whose work in medical social work, gospel music, ministry, business, and community advocacy continues to inspire and transform lives. A renowned gospel artist, minister, and businesswoman from Samburu County, she founded **Lakira House of Design** and champions boy-child empowerment, mentorship, and recovery programs.

## Music & Ministry
Nelly has released four albums featuring 25 songs, notably:
- *Mataranyaki Lkerempe*
- *Lakira Lesiran*
- *Mbingu Mpya* (Awarded Collabo Song of the Year)
- *Ikisham*

Her music has earned national recognition. She has ministered at major national events and recently celebrated a decade of music ministry through a landmark 10th Anniversary celebration that brought together gospel artists and community supporters.

## Community Impact & Professional Service
Through her professional service, faith, and community initiatives, Nelly guides young men through challenges, nurtures their potential, and promotes positive futures. Her work continues to restore hope, strengthen communities, and create lasting change in Samburu County and beyond.
