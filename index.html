Who Is Nelly?

A Leader Born to Serve and Transform

Nelly Naitengel is a multifaceted leader from Samburu County whose life stands at the intersection of faith, professional service, and community impact. As a trained Medical Social Worker, she guides vulnerable individuals and families through some of life's most difficult moments.

As a renowned Gospel Artist with over a decade of music ministry, Nelly's voice has graced national stages and moved thousands. Her business venture, Lakira House of Design, creates economic opportunities for local youth, while her CBO — Wells of Re_Birth — is a lifeline for boys and young men battling addiction and brokenness.

National Recognition

Nelly's song "Mbingu Mpya" was named Collabo Song of the Year at a national gospel awards ceremony — a testament to her artistry and the reach of her message across Kenya. She has also ministered at major national events, bringing healing and worship to thousands.
