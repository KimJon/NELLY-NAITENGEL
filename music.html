The Ministry

Singing for His Glory

Nelly Naitengel's music ministry began over a decade ago with a simple but powerful conviction — that worship could break strongholds, restore broken hearts, and transform communities. Drawing from her Samburu roots and Christian faith, she crafted a sound that is deeply authentic and widely accessible.

Over the years, she has graced stages at major national events, crusades, church conferences, and community gatherings, ministering alongside prominent gospel artists across Kenya. Her 10th Anniversary celebration was a landmark event, drawing gospel artists and fans from across the nation.

Her song "Mbingu Mpya" — a powerful collaboration — earned her the Collabo Song of the Year award nationally, cementing her place as one of Kenya's celebrated gospel voices from Samburu County.
