Event Highlights

What to Expect on 20th November

Ceremony · Empowerment

Official Launch of Wells of Re-Birth CBO

The long-awaited official commissioning of Wells of Re-Birth Boy-Child Mentorship & Recovery CBO. Witness community leaders, dignitaries, medical social workers, and families unite under the vision:

“To restore, mentor and guide the boy-child from addiction into purpose, dignity and responsible manhood through mentorship, psychosocial support and community values.”
  • Keynote speeches from Samburu County leadership and dignitaries
  • Unveiling of recovery support programs and mentorship hubs
  • Dedication prayers and community blessing
Live Worship · Celebration

Praise Jam Mega Gospel Concert

A historic gathering of worship, healing, and joy inside Kenyatta Stadium, Maralal. Free for everyone in Samburu County and visitors from across Kenya and East Africa.

John 7:38 — “Whoever believes in me, as Scripture has said, rivers of living water will flow from within them.”
  • Headline live stage ministry by Rose Muhando & Nelly Naitengel
  • Back-to-back performances by 7+ national and regional ministers
  • Powerful praise, youth empowerment testimonies, and spiritual renewal
