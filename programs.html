CBO Program

Wells of Re_Birth

Founded by Nelly Naitengel, Wells of Re_Birth is a Community-Based Organisation (CBO) with a singular focus: restoring the boy-child. In Samburu County and across Kenya, many young men are lost to addiction, brokenness, and purposelessness. Wells of Re_Birth is their lifeline.

Mission

To restore, mentor and guide the boy-child from addiction into purpose, dignity and responsible manhood through mentorship, psychosocial support and community values.

Professional Service

Medical Social Work

As a trained Medical Social Worker, Nelly provides critical psychosocial support within healthcare and community settings. Her work bridges the gap between clinical health services and the social determinants that affect wellbeing.

She works with individuals and families facing health challenges, trauma, addiction, and social crises — ensuring they receive holistic care that addresses not just physical needs but emotional, psychological, and social ones.

Psychosocial assessment and counselling
Family support and case management
Community health advocacy
Addiction recovery support
Youth and adolescent mental health
Business Enterprise

Lakira House of Design

Lakira House of Design is Nelly's creative enterprise — a business founded to foster creative expression, develop local skills, and drive economic empowerment for youth in Samburu County.

The enterprise creates a platform for local talent to flourish, connecting Samburu's rich cultural heritage with contemporary design and business opportunities.

Creative Design

Fusing Samburu culture with modern aesthetics.

Economic Empowerment

Creating jobs and opportunities for local youth.

Skills Development

Training young people in design and entrepreneurship.

Cultural Preservation

Celebrating and preserving Samburu heritage.
